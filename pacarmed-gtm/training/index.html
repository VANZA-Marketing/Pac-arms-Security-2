Kevin L. Lewis

Founder of Krav Maga Hawaiʻi and Universal Defensive Tactics (U-DTAC). A third-degree Krav Maga black belt and Certified Senior Instructor with more than 30 years of experience, Kevin trained under Darren Levine at the Krav Maga National Training Center and served eight years as Director of Training for Covered 6 Security Academy — overseeing training for thousands of security and executive-protection professionals.

  • U.S. Navy veteran & author of “The Complete Idiot’s Guide to Krav Maga”

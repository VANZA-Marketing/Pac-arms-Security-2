Our story

Built on service and integrity

Pac Armed was founded in December 2021 in Las Vegas by two local boys from Hawaiʻi — built from the ground up covering events, nightlife, and major venues like the Thomas & Mack Center. We invested early in training, then brought that same standard home to Oʻahu.

That means officers who take the work seriously, honest communication with every client, and a genuine commitment to doing what's right on each property we cover.

About Us

Built on Service. Driven by Integrity.

Pac Armed LLC was founded in Las Vegas, Nevada, in December 2021 by two local boys from Hawaiʻi with a simple belief: every client deserves to feel safe, protected, and valued.

Our Story

From a Local Vision to a Trusted Security Company

Pac Armed started from the ground up, providing security services for local events throughout the Las Vegas Valley. As our reputation for reliability, professionalism, and strong customer service grew, we expanded into the nightlife and entertainment industries, providing security for clubs, venues, private functions, and special events.

These opportunities allowed us to build experienced teams capable of working effectively in fast-paced, high-profile environments. Our performance eventually led to an important company milestone: providing security support for major events at the Thomas & Mack Center. This opportunity demonstrated our ability to manage large-scale security operations while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, accountability, and customer service.

As the company continued to grow, we made training and professional development a priority. Recognizing the increasing demand for qualified security professionals, Pac Armed began offering firearms and armed security training. These programs help future officers earn the certifications and practical skills needed to perform their duties safely, responsibly, and professionally. Training quickly became one of the foundations of our company and remains an important part of who we are today.

We also remained committed to our roots in Hawaiʻi. Pac Armed established operations on Oʻahu and formed its first Hawaiʻi partnership with the Institute for Human Services (IHS). Years later, we are proud to continue supporting IHS and its mission to assist individuals and families experiencing homelessness. This long-standing relationship reflects the types of clients and organizations we value most—those dedicated to strengthening and serving their communities.

Today, Pac Armed is a trusted security provider serving clients throughout Nevada and Hawaiʻi. After establishing a strong presence on Oʻahu, we are actively expanding our services to the outer islands. Wherever we operate, we remain committed to delivering the same professionalism, accountability, integrity, and dependable service that have defined Pac Armed from the beginning.

More than standing a post

Beyond traditional security, Pac Armed has grown into a full security and training organization serving Nevada and Hawaiʻi.
